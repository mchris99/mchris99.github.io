I designed these trophies for the National Honor Society at Bay View High School and cut them on an xTool laser from plywood and acrylic. The awards were for the top fundraisers and volunteers.

Each trophy is about 7 inches tall. I press-fit dowels through the front and back panels to hold everything together. The acrylic sits a little wider than the wood, so the edge shows.

The front panel has the BV monogram and a lattice of geometric cutouts. I went through eight versions of that pattern, from a honeycomb to an expanded triangle grid, and kept the front as one solid piece with no thin bridges that could snap. If I were to do it again, I would make the back panel match the footprint of the front panel so that light can pass through the acrylic.
