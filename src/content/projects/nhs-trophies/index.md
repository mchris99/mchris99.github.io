I designed these trophies for the National Honor Society at Bay View High School and cut them on an xTool laser from 3mm plywood and acrylic. There are awards for the top three fundraisers, the top volunteer, and the top community volunteer, with the name and award title on each one.

Each trophy is about 7 inches tall. Dowels press-fit through a front and back plywood panel and hold a sheet of acrylic between them. The acrylic sits a little wider than the wood, so its edge shows.

The front panel carries the BV monogram and a lattice of geometric cutouts. I went through eight versions of that pattern, from a honeycomb to an expanded triangle grid, and kept the front as one solid piece with no thin bridges that could snap. Test cuts showed the dowel holes needed to be 6.2 mm.
